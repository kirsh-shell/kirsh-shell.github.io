01 / THE IDEA

A shell that
understands the shape.

Kirsh starts from a simple belief: the terminal is more than a place to type commands. It is a workspace where data moves, tools compose, and small scripts become reliable systems.

The project is early and evolving. Its interfaces and implementation will be shaped in the open alongside the Kirn language.
